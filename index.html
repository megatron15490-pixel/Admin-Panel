About this prototype

Version 0.1.0 · Browser demo

Nexora is a learning prototype inspired by school device-management consoles.

Demo devices, test policies, and demo activity are stored in this browser using localStorage. Your personal display name is stored separately in Supabase after setup. Clearing browser site data may erase demo data. Device status and policy decisions are simulated and are not connected to school systems.
